# G3: The database/sql driver

Status: next, in one round with F8 and Q1. Planned at 2 hours. It needs P4,
which is done.

## The contract

From BETA.md: the private database/sql driver behind `Query`, `QueryRow`,
`Exec` and `SQL()`, with the transaction carried in the context, results
copied out and `Begin` refused. Done when every value and destination type
scans as it does in 0.x, and `SQL().Begin` returns an error.

## What's there already

- **The public package** (P4 to G2), in `beta/hypercrux`: `Query`,
  `QueryRow`, `Exec` and `SQL()` on `DB` and `Tx` are stubs, behind
  `stubSQL`, which P4.md's note says a `*sql.DB` over the Beta's driver
  replaces, made in `Open` and closed by `Close`.
- **The rules,** in `beta/SQL.md`: "Arguments" and "Results" under
  "Values", "Statements" for `Exec`, `Query`, `RowsAffected` and
  `LastInsertId`, and "Errors and their kinds".
- **0.x's driver use,** in `sqlapi.go` in the repository's root, over
  go-sqlite3, with the transaction in the context.
- **The parser** (Q3): `Params()` gives the driver's `NumInput`, and the
  check for too few or too many arguments is the driver's.
- **Running SQL** is G4's, once the operators and the planner are in. Until
  then the driver's tests can hand it rows of values directly.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The driver,** kept private to the package, with a connection for each
  `DB`. `SQL()` gives its `*sql.DB`, with `Begin` refused.
- **The transaction in the context,** so a `Tx`'s `Query` runs inside its
  `Update` and sees its changes.
- **Arguments in,** as SQL.md's table has them, NaN as NULL and a time as
  go-sqlite3's text among them, and **values out,** copied, with a vector
  as its bytes (P3.md's note).
- **Errors:** 0.x's kinds through `Query`, `QueryRow` and `Exec`, and none
  wrapped through `SQL()`, as P5.md's note says.
- **The tests:** every value kind scanned into every destination type
  database/sql takes, on the Beta and on 0.x side by side, with the same
  results and the same errors. Beside that, `SQL().Begin` refused, and a
  `Tx`'s query seeing its own changes, as far as G3 can go before G4 runs
  SQL.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a vector handed out that still shares the
  store's memory, or a time bound in another layout, each with a line in
  `beta/plants.txt`.
