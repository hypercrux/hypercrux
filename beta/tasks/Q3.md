## The contract

From BETA.md: the parser: the whole subset, with the walk forms, the
one-record subquery, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE` and parameters. Done when
every corpus statement parses or is refused as marked, and a fuzz of the
parser runs clean.

## What's there already

- **The subset,** in `beta/SQL.md`: the statements, words and symbols,
  keywords, names, literals and parameters, the grammar, the operators and
  how they group, a sign before a literal, names and columns, what a result
  column is called, and the walk forms. "Where the Beta differs from 0.x"
  lists what's refused that 0.x runs.
- **The corpus** (A4), in `beta/sqlcorpus/testdata`: statements marked in or
  out of the subset, 3,000 generated expressions and 600 date cases, with
  0.x's answers. Every statement marked out says why.
- **The query package** (P3), in `beta/internal/query`: the operator
  iterator, `Rows`, and nothing else yet.
- **The errors** (P3), in `beta/internal/errs`, with SQL.md's "Errors and
  their kinds" for which kind each SQL error is.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **Where it lives** in `beta/internal/query`, and the tree it gives for
  statements and expressions, which Q1 evaluates and the later Q tasks build
  on. Keep what evaluation needs, such as a literal's exact text for reals
  and SQL.md's rule for a sign before a literal.
- **Refusals:** a statement outside the subset is an error of SQL.md's kind,
  with a message that says what isn't taken, before anything runs.
- **Result column names,** as SQL.md's "What a result column is called"
  gives them.
- **Parameters:** `?` only, counted, and the errors for too many or too few
  arguments as SQL.md has them.
- **The tests:** every case in the corpus parses or is refused as marked; a
  test of each grammar rule and each refusal; printing a tree back as SQL
  and parsing it again gives the same tree; and a fuzz target, which
  `scripts/fuzz.sh` and CI's fuzz job pick up by themselves.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a wrong precedence or a keyword taken as a
  name, each with a line in `beta/plants.txt`.
