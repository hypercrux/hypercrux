# F5: Failed commits

Status: next, in one round with V1 and T5. Planned at 2 hours. It needs F3,
which is done.

## The contract

From BETA.md: failed commits: a cut back to just after the last marker, or
else the lock kept and writes refused. Done when T3 passes with a failure
at every call, and a handle stuck that way blocks other writers until it's
closed.

## What's there already

- **The rules,** in FORMAT.md under "Writing": a writer whose commit fails
  at any step cuts the file back to just after the last marker and syncs it
  before it lets go of the lock, and if that fails too, it keeps the lock
  and writes nothing more until the database is closed. "Checking the end of
  the log" handles a failure in the check like a failed commit, for now.
- **The log** (F2 to F4), in `beta/internal/logfile`. `failed` is the one
  place for Append's failures and the check's. `stuck` already keeps a
  Log's lock until `Close`. Until now a failure leaves its bytes where they
  are, `catchUp` skips them, and Append refuses to write after them;
  `TestAFailedCommitStopsAppends` and
  `TestAFailedCheckIsHandledLikeAFailedCommit` pin that.
- **The error,** `errs.ErrStuck`, exported by the public package, for a
  handle that refuses every write until it's closed.
- **The driver** (T3), in `beta/internal/crash`: `Options{Kinds:
  crash.Failures}`, and `Times: -1` for a disk that dies, so the cut back
  fails too. `TestTheRealLogAfterAFailedCall` in `crash_test.go` is where
  the closing test goes.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The cut back:** to just after the last marker, then synced, with the
  last marker written again first, as `markLeft` does, for F3.md's reason.
- **The handle that sticks:** when the cut back fails, the lock stays held
  and every write through the handle fails with `ErrStuck` until it's
  closed, while other handles and processes wait for the lock.
- **A failure while the check writes a batch again:** F3.md shows how the
  rule as it stands could cut a batch whose commit may have succeeded.
  Keeping the lock, or leaving the batch for the next check, keeps it.
  Settle which, and give FORMAT.md's wording.
- **What goes:** the skip in `catchUp` and Append's refusal. The two tests
  that pin them change to the new behaviour.
- **The tests:** the driver with a failure at every call, on workloads that
  carry on after a failed commit, and again with `Times: -1`; and a stuck
  handle blocking another process's writer until it's closed. A reader that
  races a failed commit's cut, as F4.md suggests, can join them.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a failed commit left uncut, or a stuck handle
  that lets go of the lock, each with a line in `beta/plants.txt`.
