Status: next, in one round with S4 and G1. Planned at 4 hours. It needs F2

## The contract

From BETA.md: crash recovery: the tail check, a complete batch rewritten,
synced and marked, a torn marker rewritten, a cut from the first bad batch.
Done when T3 passes with a cut at every write.

  steps in their order and the table of what each end means.
- **The log** (F2), in `beta/internal/logfile`, which stops at the end of
  the log and refuses to append after a tail it hasn't checked. F2.md's
  note under F3 says where the check goes: in `lockFile`, after `readOn`,

  replaying exactly unless tests can fix it.
- **Names at a cut** (T2): the fault layer now loses unsynced changes to
  names, so the creation of a database is tested as FORMAT.md sets it out.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The check,** steps 2 and 3: a batch that counts with no whole marker
  after it written again, synced and marked; then everything after the
  last marker cut off, and the cut synced. A torn marker after a counting
  batch is the same case.
- **Step 1, looking past the end of the log for markers, is F4's.** Leave
  its place, and say in this file what the check does until it lands.
- **Who runs the check:** the holder of the write lock before it appends,
  opening when it gets the lock without waiting, and a reader that finds
  the writer gone (F6 later).
- **A fixed database ID for tests,** so a crash point replays.
- **The damage named twice:** `readOn` wraps an error from `Target.Apply`
  in a `*errs.Damage` of its own, where the store's error is one already
  (S3.md). Take the store's, as the codec's is taken, with `errors.As`.
- **The tests:** T3's driver on the real log with a cut at every write, with
  enough seeds to find `logfile/marker-before-sync` (T3.md says a hundred or
  more), and the cases in FORMAT.md's table that recovery handles.
