Status: next, in one round with S5 and F4. Planned at 3 hours. It needs P4

## The contract

From BETA.md: the many-process harness: writer and reader processes,
killed at random. Done when it catches a planted reordering bug.

## What's there already

- **What the harness is for:** F6 passes its closing test through it,
  every reader seeing every commit in order with writers killed at random,
  and F9 does the same through compactions and a backup moved into place.
  I1 then runs the many-process suites through the public API.
- **Processes from the test binary,** in `beta/conformance/process.go`:
  `Main` sends a copy of the test binary to its role by an environment
  variable, and the parent kills writers with SIGKILL at random moments.
  That's 0.x's way, and the suite runs it on the Beta once the public
  package has links and vectors.
- **The crash-point driver** (T3), in `beta/internal/crash`, for the shape
  of a harness that a test hands a workload and a model, and a toy log of
  its own in its tests. T3.md's note under T4 says why readers beside a
  writer need real processes: goroutines making file calls side by side
  don't replay.
- **The real log** (F2 and F3), in `beta/internal/logfile`. It commits
  under the mutex and `flock`, and the next holder of the lock marks or
  cuts what a killed writer left (F3.md's note under T4). A reader sees
  other processes' commits only by opening the file again until F6 adds
  following.
- **Planted bugs** (P1): `plant_off.go` and `plant_on.go`, and a line in
  `beta/plants.txt`.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **Where it lives,** as a package of its own, and its API: what a test
  hands it (the writers' and readers' work, and what each reports back) and
  what it checks.
- **What it checks:** each reader sees every commit exactly once and in
  order, and nothing a writer didn't commit; every reader agrees on what
  each commit holds; a commit a writer saw succeed is in the file at the
  end; and a killed writer's commit under way is in the file whole or not
  at all.
- **Killing:** writers and readers alike, with SIGKILL at random moments,
  and new ones started in their place, for a time or a count, shorter
  under `-short`. The seed is logged, though process timing won't replay.
- **How children report** to the parent, so a kill never leaves half a
  report that reads as a whole one.
- **The toy engine** in its tests, small enough to be plainly right
  without the bug, and its planted reordering bug, which only another
  process can see, such as a writer that puts its marker down before its
  batch, or a reader that takes a batch before its marker. The test has to
  catch it in every run, within a few seconds.
- **A run on the real log,** with readers that open the file afresh, as
  far as the time allows, since following is F6's.
