# F4: Damage

Status: next, in one round with S5 and T4. Planned at 2 hours. It needs F3,
which is done.

## The contract

From BETA.md: damage: reported in the middle with nothing cut, a look past
zeros before cutting, a bad marked batch read again under the lock. Done
when every damaged fixture is reported and nothing is cut.

## What's there already

- **The rules,** in FORMAT.md: "Looking past the end of the log", step 1
  of "Checking the end of the log" and the rows of its table that are
  damage, and under "Reading the log" what opening does and what a reader
  does with a marked batch that fails its checks. Damage is confirmed by a
  read under the write lock, since what lies past the end of the log can
  change while a writer is at work.
- **The log** (F2 and F3), in `beta/internal/logfile`. The comment at the
  top of `log.go` says where F4 goes, and each place has a comment that
  starts with F4: at the end of `readOn`, before `tryCheck` in `Open`, and
  at the top of `checkEnd`, before `markLeft` writes anything. The rule
  that a log ending inside the compacted part is damage is in `checkEnd`
  already, for holders of the lock.
- **The cases waiting for F4:** the four marked `f4` in `endCases` in
  `check_test.go`, and two in `TestReadingStopsAtTheEndOfTheLog`, "a marked
  batch that fails its checksum" and "a marker naming the batch before",
  which F3.md's decisions place as step 2's damage.
- **The codec** (F1): `DecodeMarker` checks the 4-byte magic number before
  anything else, so it can be tried at every offset.
- **The crash tests** (F3), in `crash_test.go`, with 128 seeds for a new
  database so that `logfile/marker-before-sync` can be caught by cuts once
  step 1 is in.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The look past the end of the log,** wherever the log is read to its
  end, holding the lock or not: how it finds the marker magic quickly in a
  long tail, and the batch and offset its `*errs.Damage` names.
- **Confirming under the lock:** a reader that finds a whole marker past
  the end, or a marked batch that fails its checks, waits for the write
  lock as a writer does and reads again before it reports damage. Opening
  that doesn't get the lock at once is such a reader.
- **The compacted-part rule for readers,** as well as for holders of the
  lock.
- **The damaged fixtures:** a damaged file for each kind of damage in
  FORMAT.md, in the middle of the log as well as at its end, each with what
  must be reported. Opening each one, and locking it, has to report its
  damage and leave its bytes as they were. They go where G7's `check` can
  use them too.
- **The cases waiting for F4** turned into damage, and the crash tests
  catching `logfile/marker-before-sync` by cuts.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a cut that doesn't look past zeros first, or
  damage reported without the read under the lock, each with a line in
  `beta/plants.txt`.
