# V1: Vector arrays and Nearest

Status: next, in one round with T5 and F5. Planned at 4 hours. It needs S2
and A5, which are done.

## The contract

From BETA.md: vector arrays and `Nearest`: arrays without pointers, float64
norms, free slots, the size set by the first vector and reset by `Drop`,
top k with ties broken by key, a filter hook. Done when the model test
passes and results match a brute-force search.

## What's there already

- **The design,** in BETA.md under "In memory" and "Vectors": each table's
  vectors back to back in one block that holds no pointers, their norms in
  float64 in an array of their own, a mark on every free slot, and a search
  that streams through the array, skipping free slots and records the
  filter rules out, with the closest k kept in a heap. The Targets table
  gives 65 ms for 10 nearest among 100,000 vectors of 384 values, 12 ms
  with a tenth passing a filter, and 26 ms among 10,000 of 1,536.
- **The search loop** (A5), in `beta/internal/vecmath`: the dot product in
  8 running sums and the distance from the dot product and the two norms,
  with the same bits on amd64 and arm64.
- **The read API** (P3), in `read.go`: `Nearest(table, q, k, keep)`, with k
  from 1 to 10,000, a table without a vector size giving no hits, and the
  `Filter` called once for each record with a vector, before its distance,
  maybe from more than one goroutine. `Record.Vec` is the record's slot.
  `Nearest` is a stub in `store.go` that names V1.
- **The store** (S1 to S5), in `beta/internal/store`. A record's vector is
  a slice of its own for now, which `record.vec` notes is V1's to make a
  slot. The table's vector size is set by its first vector and goes with a
  drop. Every change has an undo entry (S2), and the snapshot copies each
  vector out with `value.Vector` (S3).
- **0.x's search,** in `vectors.go` in the repository's root, with its
  checks, its ties and its errors, and the conformance suite's `Nearest`
  cases.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The arrays:** the layout of a table's vectors and norms, how they grow,
  how free slots are marked and used again, and the memory a vector takes
  beside its values.
- **The writes:** a put that sets a vector or clears it, a delete freeing
  its slot, and a drop taking the table's array and size with it. Each with
  undo entries, so a rollback puts the old values back, and a slot freed in
  a transaction stays out of use until the commit (S2.md's note). Norms are
  worked out at the write.
- **`Nearest`:** on the store and through a transaction, which sees its own
  changes, with 0.x's checks and errors in 0.x's order. Top k with ties
  broken by key, and the filter hook.
- **Opening:** `LoadBatch` putting vectors straight into the arrays, with
  their norms, since the open target counts them.
- **The tests:** the model test with vectors, including through transactions
  and across rollbacks; results against a brute-force search on random
  vectors and awkward ones, such as very small values and vectors nearly the
  same; 0.x's `Nearest` cases run against the store and 0.x side by side;
  and a rough timing of the three searches in the Targets table, written
  down for G2 and I1.
- **Planted bugs** for vectors and searches, each with a line in
  `beta/plants.txt`.
