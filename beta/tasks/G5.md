## What's there already

- **The command suite** (A1), in `beta/conformance/cmdtest`: 0.x's tests of
  the `hypercrux` command, run from outside against the binary
  `HYPERCRUX_BIN` names, with `HYPERCRUX_CMD_SKIP` for the sections to
  leave out. SQL.md's named tests list its sections, with SQLTriggers and
  AdoptAndDrop left out, so the `drop` verb needs a test of its own
  (P5.md's note).
- **0.x's command,** in `cmd/hypercrux` at the root, with its verbs, its
  output, its messages and its exit codes. P3.md puts the Beta's at
  `beta/cmd/hypercrux`.
- **The public package:** every call works, and `Compact` is the one stub
  left.
- **Compaction in the log** (F8): `Compact` and `Due`. F8.md's note gives
  the shape of `Compact()` and of the compaction after a commit, which
  `Update` runs while it still holds the lock. F9.md's notes say other
  processes reload by themselves, and F7.md's that the file rules can
  refuse a compaction. I1's `CompactThroughTheDB` in
  `export_workloads_test.go` is the shape of `Compact`.
- **What the verbs need:** G6.md's note on `import` and `export`, G7.md's on
  `check`, G4.md's on the command's SQL, P4.md's on the version line, and
  P5.md's and Q3.md's on one statement with exit code 2.
- **A finding from I1,** in the log: a commit after a compaction whose
  folder sync failed can be lost at a power cut, once the stuck handle has
  closed. `TestACommitAfterAStuckCompactionLasts` shows it and is skipped.
  I1.md's note has a fix: before its first commit into a file of
  generation 2 or more, a Log syncs the database's folder once.
- **Opening:** about 9 µs a commit on a log of small commits (G4.md), which
  the compaction after a commit keeps in bounds.
