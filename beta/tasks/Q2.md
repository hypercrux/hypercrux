# Q2: Dates

Status: next, in one round with F9 and Q4. Planned at 2 hours. It needs P3
and P5, which are done, and uses Q3's parser and Q1's evaluator.

## The contract

From BETA.md: dates: `date` and `datetime` on `'now'`, plus or minus days,
months and years. Done when A4's date cases, month ends included, match
exactly.

## What's there already

- **The rules,** in `beta/SQL.md` under "Dates": `date('now' [, m ...])` and
  `datetime('now' [, m ...])` in UTC, modifiers of days, months and years,
  a day past the end of its month running on into the next as SQLite does
  it, NULL where SQLite gives NULL, and 'now' read once for each statement.
  SQL.md names SQLite's routines: `parseModifier`, `computeJD`,
  `computeYMD`, `dateFunc` and `datetimeFunc`.
- **The corpus** (A4), in `beta/sqlcorpus/testdata/dates.jsonl`: 608 cases
  on fixed dates, with month ends and leap days, and 0.x's answers. They're
  outside the subset, which takes 'now' only, so they test the arithmetic.
  The statements corpus has the 'now' cases.
- **The parser** (Q3) gives `date()` and `datetime()` with text literals
  only, and the fixed-date cases parse, so the test can take their calls
  from the tree (Q3.md's note under Q2).
- **The evaluator** (Q1), in `beta/internal/query`: `date()` and
  `datetime()` go through `Scope.Call` for now. Q1.md's note under Q2 says
  how to work them out in `funcs.go` instead, with the statement's 'now' in
  a new field of `Frame`.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The arithmetic,** as SQLite's routines do it: days, months and years in
  turn, the month ends, leap days, and the range where SQLite gives NULL.
- **'now':** read once for each statement, and handed to the evaluator so
  every row sees the same moment.
- **Refusals:** a first argument other than 'now', and a modifier outside
  SQL.md's pattern, refused before anything runs, with a message that says
  what isn't taken.
- **The tests:** every date case in the corpus gives 0.x's answer exactly,
  through the arithmetic on its fixed date, and so do random dates and
  modifiers run against 0.x through `zerox`, in a file built only with
  cgo. The 'now' cases are checked against 0.x at the time, as the corpus
  checks them.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a month end that clamps where SQLite runs on,
  each with a line in `beta/plants.txt`.
