# T3: Crash-point driver

Status: next, in one round with S3 and T2. Planned at 3 hours. It needs T1
and P1, which are done.

## The contract

From BETA.md: the crash-point driver: a cut at every write and a failure at
every call, then a reopen compared with the model, plus a copy taken
mid-commit. Done when it catches a planted "marker before sync" bug in a
toy log.

## What's there already

- **The fault layer for data** (T1), in `beta/internal/fault`. T1.md's note
  under T3 sets out the driver's loop: count the calls on `New(seed)`, then
  for each one run again on a fresh disk with a rule that cuts or fails it,
  `Clear` the rules, and check through a new `FS()`. A copy taken
  mid-commit needs a thin `fsys.FS` of T3's own over the disk's.
- **What the driver will run:** F3's recovery, F5's failed commits and F8's
  compaction pass their closing tests through it, on the real log in
  `beta/internal/logfile` (F2). That log can't recover yet, so this task's
  test is a toy log of its own.
- **Planted bugs** (P1): `plant_off.go` and `plant_on.go`, and a line in
  `beta/plants.txt`. F2 already plants `logfile/marker-before-sync` in the
  real log, which this driver can catch once F3 is in.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The driver's API:** what a test gives it (a workload, a way to reopen
  and read the state back, a model of the commits that succeeded) and what
  it checks after each cut or failure: the state is the last commit that
  succeeded, or the one under way.
- **Where it lives,** as a package of its own, and how F3, F5 and F8 will
  call it.
- **The copy mid-commit:** taken at a call the driver picks, read as `cp`
  would read it, and opened like any database.
- **The toy log:** small, with a batch, a sync and a marker, and recovery
  enough to check it. Its planted bug writes the marker before the sync.
- **The tests:** the driver catching the planted bug, and passing the toy
  log without it, with cuts at every write and failures at every call.
