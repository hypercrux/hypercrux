## The contract

From BETA.md: the planner: a nearest search for `ORDER BY distance` with
`LIMIT`, rows without a vector first otherwise, walk joins, `IN` and
`NOT IN` over walks, the one-record subquery. Done when plan-shape tests
pass, and the crux query matches 0.x on fixed random data.

  Q4.md (no FROM, walk tables, joins, subqueries and IN over walks), with

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The planner:** a statement's tree to a plan of Q4's operators over the
  store, with names found as SQL.md says, and every expression compiled
  before any row runs (Q2.md's note).
- **The nearest search:** `ORDER BY distance(vec, ?)` with a LIMIT becomes
  the store's `Nearest`, with WHERE as its filter, raising `distance()`'s
  errors exactly where 0.x would (P5.md's note). Without the LIMIT, rows
  without a vector come first.
- **Walks:** the walk as a table, joins over it, `IN` and `NOT IN` over
  walks, and `walk()` as a function, with a key that isn't a record
  reaching nothing (S5.md's note).
- **The one-record subquery,** worked out once for each statement.
- **What's left for Q6 and G4:** writes, and joining the planner to the
  driver.
- **The tests:** plan-shape tests for each case in the contract, and the
  crux query on fixed random data against 0.x through `zerox`: the closing
  test. Beside them, every SELECT in the corpus giving 0.x's answers
  through the planner.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a nearest search that ignores the filter or a
  walk join that drops a depth, each with a line in `beta/plants.txt`.
