Status: next, in one round with Q1 and G3. Planned at 3 hours. It needs F5

## The contract

From BETA.md: compaction: `NAME.compact` with the database's permissions,
sync, rename, directory sync, cleanup on failure, writers waiting it out.
Done when T3 passes with a cut at every write and a failure at every call
during a compaction, and a copy taken mid-compaction opens.

## What's there already

- **The design,** in BETA.md under "Compaction": the steps, from creating
  `NAME.compact` and taking `flock` on it to the rename that is the switch
  and the directory sync after it; what a failure before the switch and
  after it leaves; a leftover `NAME.compact` removed by the next holder of
  the write lock; and writers that wait past the usual 10 seconds while
  `NAME.compact` is locked.
- **The format,** in FORMAT.md under "Compaction": the header's next
  generation and its last four fields, the compacted part's order of
  tables, records and links, batches of the writer's choosing, and the
  header written last.
- **The snapshot** (S3 to V1), in `beta/internal/store`: `Snapshot` gives the
  whole copy as the changes of a compacted part, in FORMAT.md's order, one
  change at a time. `TestSnapshotOfTheFixtures` is a golden test for it:
  compacting `file-new.hex` has to write `file-compacted.hex`'s compacted
  part.
- **The log** (F2 to F6), in `beta/internal/logfile`: creation's steps,
  which a compaction's file can share, `stuck` for a directory sync that
  fails after the switch, Lock's wait for `flock` that reads on between its
  tries (F6), and the inode check that moves a writer to the new file.
- **The fault layer** (T1 and T2) and **the driver** (T3), with names lost
  or kept at a cut, and T2.md's note on the switch.

## To settle, and write down in this file

- **The log's compaction:** the call that compacts, holding the write lock,
  from a snapshot handed to it as changes, since the log and the store
  don't import each other, written in batches of about 1 MiB up to the
  switch.
- **When it runs:** BETA.md's rule of thumb, a file about twice the live
  data, and how the log can tell. The public package's `Compact()` and the
  automatic compaction after a commit are G5's to join up, since G3 works
  in `beta/hypercrux` this round. Say what they need.
- **Failures:** before the switch, `NAME.compact` removed and the old file
  carried on with; after the rename, a failed directory sync sticks the
  handle (F5.md's note).
- **Writers waiting it out,** in this process and others, past the usual 10
  seconds while `NAME.compact` is locked, then moving to the new file.
- **The tests:** T3's driver with a cut at every write and a failure at
  every call during a compaction, and a copy taken mid-compaction that
  opens; compacting the fixture gives `file-compacted.hex`'s compacted
  part; a leftover `NAME.compact` removed; and the rate at which a
  compaction writes, measured roughly, for BETA.md's estimate.
- **Planted bugs,** such as a rename before the sync, or a leftover removed
  before the inode check, each with a line in `beta/plants.txt`.
