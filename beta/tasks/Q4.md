## The contract

From BETA.md: operators: scan, filter, project, sort, top k, limit and
offset, aggregates over the whole result. Done when each operator passes
against a fake store, and aggregates match A4's answers.

## What's there already

- **The rules,** in `beta/SQL.md` under "Queries": FROM, the order of rows,
  WHERE, result columns, aggregates, ORDER BY, LIMIT and OFFSET. Under
  "Functions", the aggregates, with Kahan-Babuska-Neumaier summation in the
  order of rows (P5.md's note). Sorts are stable.
- **The operator iterator** (P3), `query.Rows`, and the store's read API,
  `store.Reader`, which a fake can implement. A table's scan is
  `r.Scan(table+":", "")`, in key order (S4.md's note).
- **The tree** (Q3) and **the evaluator** (Q1), in `beta/internal/query`.
  Q1.md's note under Q4 says how aggregates reach `Scope.Call`, how an
  operator can read each aggregate's result from `Frame.Row`, and that
  `compare` and `truth` are SQLite's order and truth.
- **The corpus** (A4): the statements with aggregates, with 0.x's answers
  on the small fixed database that `sqlcorpus.Fixture` builds.
